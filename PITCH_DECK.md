## 1. BRIEF ABOUT THE SOLUTION

**Pramaan** (ಪ್ರಮಾಣ - *The Proof / Intelligence*) is an enterprise-grade AI-powered **Police Intelligence Command Platform** engineered specifically for **Karnataka State Police (KSP)** and Indian law enforcement agencies.

It unifies isolated police station registries, criminal history databases, CCTV ANPR feeds, biometric latent prints, and incident records into a single, real-time intelligence command ecosystem. Powered by **Zoho Catalyst (ZCQL + AppSail)**, **Vector RAG**, **Fellegi-Sunter Identity Resolution**, **MeitY Bhashini Multilingual Speech AI**, and **Google Satellite GEOINT Maps**, Pramaan enables police officers, crime analysts, and policymakers to reconstruct crime scenarios, map precise crime locations, detect cross-district serial crime rings in seconds, analyze socio-demographic risk factors, project 30-day crime spikes, resolve suspect identities, enhance smudged latent prints, simulate fugitive aging, and generate **Court-Ready Case Files & Form 54 Case Diaries**.

## 4. HOW WILL IT BE ABLE TO SOLVE THE PROBLEM?

1. **Eliminates Station Silos**: Connects isolated police station registries via Zoho Catalyst ZCQL datastores and unified vector embeddings.
2. **Reconstructs Crime Scenarios & Maps Locations**: Identifies exact crime incident locations on Google Satellite GEOINT maps and reconstructs spatial/temporal modus operandi scenarios.
3. **Generates Court-Ready Case Files**: Auto-generates certified **Form 54 Case Diaries** under Sec 172 Cr.P.C. / Sec 193 BNSS / Sec 65B BSA with digital officer attestations.
4. **Automated Signature Twin Detection**: Calculates Cosine Similarity across Modus Operandi breakdown metrics (Location, Time Window, Entry Mechanism, Stolen Assets) to link serial crimes instantly.
5. **Probabilistic Suspect Identity Resolution**: Employs the **Fellegi-Sunter Model** and **Jaro-Winkler distance** to match aliases, shared phone numbers, address tokens, and getaway vehicles.
6. **Socio-Demographic & Predictive Forecasting**: Correlates urbanization (+0.84), youth unemployment (+0.91), and economic stress indices to project 30-day crime spikes.
